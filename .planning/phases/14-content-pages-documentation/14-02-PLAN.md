**Section 1: Getting Started (ONBOARD-03 beta guide)**
Heading: "Getting Started"
FAQ items (AccordionItem with AccordionTrigger + AccordionContent):
- "What is PriCal?" — PriCal automatically protects your Calendly links by cancelling bookings from people not on your approved allowlist. Set it up once, and it works 24/7.
- "How do I set up PriCal?" — Step-by-step guide: (1) Sign in with your Calendly account, (2) Add emails to your allowlist - these are the people allowed to book with you, (3) Set up the Calendly webhook - go to your Calendly account > Integrations > Webhooks, create a webhook pointing to `https://prical.com/api/webhooks/calendly` for the "invitee.created" event, (4) You're protected - PriCal will now automatically screen new bookings.
- "What are the known limitations?" — List: (a) PriCal only works with Calendly (no Google Calendar or Outlook direct integration), (b) Webhook delivery depends on Calendly's infrastructure - occasional delays of a few seconds are normal, (c) Cancelled bookings cannot be automatically re-created if someone is later added to the allowlist, (d) CSV import is limited to Pro+ plans, (e) Beta: some features are still being refined - please report issues.
- "How do I report bugs or give feedback?" — Link to GitHub issues: "Visit our [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/prical/prical/issues) to report bugs or suggest features. You can also email us at support@prical.com."

**Section 2: How-To Guides**
Heading: "How-To Guides"
FAQ items:
- "How do I add someone to my allowlist?" — Go to the Allowlist page from the dashboard sidebar. Click "Add Email" and enter the person's email address. They'll be immediately approved for future bookings.
- "How do I import emails in bulk?" — Pro+ users can use CSV import. Go to the Allowlist page, click "Import CSV", and upload a CSV file with email addresses. Duplicates are automatically skipped and invalid emails are reported.
- "How do I export my allowlist?" — On the Allowlist page, click "Export CSV" to download all your allowlist entries as a CSV file including email, name, notes, and date added.
- "How do I change my email notification settings?" — Go to Settings from the dashboard sidebar. Under Email Preferences, toggle notifications for approved bookings, rejected bookings, and trial warnings.
- "How do I set up the Calendly webhook?" — In your Calendly account, go to Integrations > Webhooks. Create a new webhook subscription with the URL `https://prical.com/api/webhooks/calendly` and select the "invitee.created" event. PriCal will start receiving booking notifications immediately.

**Section 3: Pricing FAQ**
Heading: "Pricing FAQ"
FAQ items:
- "Is there a free plan?" — Yes. The Free plan includes basic allowlist management and booking protection. Upgrade to Pro for CSV import, extended audit logs, and priority support.
- "How does the free trial work?" — New accounts start with a 14-day Pro trial. You get full access to all Pro features during the trial. When it ends, your account moves to the Free plan unless you subscribe.
- "How do I upgrade or cancel my subscription?" — Go to Settings from the dashboard sidebar. Click "Manage Subscription" to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel through our secure Stripe billing portal.
- "What payment methods do you accept?" — We accept all major credit and debit cards through Stripe. All payments are processed securely.

**Section 4: Troubleshooting**
Heading: "Troubleshooting"
FAQ items:
- "Why aren't my bookings being screened?" — Check three things: (1) Your Calendly webhook is correctly configured and pointing to PriCal, (2) Your allowlist has at least one email entry, (3) Your account is active (not expired trial with no subscription).
- "I added someone to my allowlist but their booking was still cancelled" — Allowlist changes apply to future bookings only. If the booking was already processed before you added the email, it cannot be automatically restored. Ask the person to rebook.
- "I'm not receiving email notifications" — Check your email notification settings in Settings > Email Preferences. Make sure the relevant notification types are enabled. Also check your spam/junk folder.
- "My CSV import failed" — Ensure your CSV file has one email per row (or an "email" column header). Check that emails are valid formats. The maximum supported file is 500 rows. CSV import requires a Pro+ plan.
